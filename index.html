01 — DEFINE

Start with the reality.

Don't begin by asking what you should manifest. Define the reality that would meaningfully change your life.

Desire

What do I actually want?

Why

Why does it matter?

End State

What does fulfilled reality look like?

The objective is not merely to desire an outcome. It is to define a reality that can be translated into measurable evidence.
02 — IDENTITY ENGINE

Become the source of the outcome.

Reverse-engineer the person who naturally produces the reality you want.

I AM

Builder.
Owner.
Researcher.
Operator.
Problem solver.
Long-term thinker.

Standards

I don't wait for motivation. I don't confuse temporary failure with identity. I don't consume endlessly without producing.

Don't manifest the outcome. Manifest the identity capable of repeatedly producing the outcome.
03 — ASSUMPTION ENGINE

Install the operating beliefs.

Instead of: “I hope I become successful.”


Use: “I am someone who figures things out.”

Instead of: “I need an opportunity.”


Use: “Valuable problems are everywhere; my job is to identify them.”

Instead of: “I'm behind.”


Use: “My trajectory matters more than my starting point.”

04 — STATE ENGINE

Make the future psychologically familiar.

Enter the state associated with the identity. The target isn't necessarily excitement. It is calm familiarity.

Certainty

“I can handle this.”

Abundance

“Possibilities exist.”

Ownership

“My next action is mine to choose.”

05 — ATTENTION ENGINE

If my desired reality were already emerging, what would I notice today?

Problems

Leverage

What can be automated, delegated, packaged or compounded?

Signals

What evidence suggests the trajectory is improving?

07 — IF / THEN ENGINE

Automate the identity.

IF I feel resistance → THEN work for 10 minutes before deciding whether to stop.
IF I discover a promising idea → THEN test it before fantasizing about it.
IF an experiment fails → THEN record why and run the next experiment.
IF I see an opportunity → THEN investigate it.
IF I become distracted → THEN return to the highest-leverage task.
08 — EVIDENCE ENGINE

Prove the identity through action.

Create an Evidence Ledger. Every day, collect observable proof that the identity is becoming real.

Builder

I built for two hours.

Disciplined

I completed the hardest task despite resistance.

Researcher

I completed and logged one experiment.
